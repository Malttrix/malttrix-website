01 / THE REASON WE EXIST

More information. Less clarity.

Knowledge is everywhere. Knowing what deserves your attention is another matter. A saved article, an unopened course, one more clever shortcut: information can accumulate faster than understanding.

Immersation connects ideas, examines their limits and gives useful knowledge a structure you can return to. The aim is a clearer relationship with your energy, work and resources.
