01 / THE BEGINNING

A curious mind. A love for art.

SOUND ↔ VISION

I have always been curious about unfamiliar ideas. Music and visual art gave that curiosity an emotional language: a melody could offer comfort, and an image could make an ordinary moment feel different.

Art became more than entertainment to me. It could connect people and give difficult feelings a form. That belief stayed with me as I began to imagine creating something of my own.

Yet many ideas never get the conditions they need to grow. I wanted to understand how imagination could be explored freely, shaped with care, and brought into the world.

Some feelings are difficult to explain. Art gives them another way to be expressed.
02 / THE TURNING POINT

When imagination needed more room.

Working life taught me discipline, accountability and organised thinking. I valued those foundations, but began to notice how easily an unfamiliar idea could be limited by an established framework.

There were possibilities I wanted to explore, and different ways to approach a problem. There was not always room to try them. I began to ask myself a question:

What would it feel like to create without constantly having to fit my imagination into someone else’s boundaries?

I did not want to reject responsibility. I wanted a space where art, technology and storytelling could meet without having to fit a single existing model.

And I realised that if I couldn’t find the creative space I was looking for, perhaps I could build one myself.

I didn’t want to spend my life only imagining what could be. I wanted to discover what I could create.
03 / WHY I FOUNDED MALTTRIX

A place where imagination can take form.

IMAGINATION → FORM

I founded Malttrix to give original ideas a home: an independent place where different projects could develop distinct identities, supported by a shared foundation.

Music could remain music. Visual art could tell its own stories. Knowledge could help someone see differently. The purpose was to give each expression room to mature.

Malttrix connects these worlds through creative direction, digital systems and patient stewardship, so an idea can become work that reaches beyond its starting point.

It is a growing creative journey, built through exploration, experimentation and the belief that a small idea deserves the chance to become meaningful.

Born from imagination. Refined into form.

TWO INSTINCTS / ONE INTEGRATED PRACTICE

Founder · Creative Systems Architect

Connecting artistic direction, original IP, technology and operations so independent creative work can develop.

Creative Systems Architect describes how I connect artistic direction with the practical structures that help an idea become an experience.

In practice, I shape a brand’s point of view, connect its identity to its content and digital presence, and design a process that supports consistent creative work.

AI and digital tools can support research, production and everyday operations. I use technology to make more room for human judgment and creative intent.

A creative idea does not need the most complicated system. It needs the right one.
