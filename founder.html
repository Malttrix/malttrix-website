MAXON TAN

Founder & Creative Systems Architect

The person behind Malttrix.

I’ve always been drawn to two different worlds.

One is built on logic, structure, and the need to understand how things work. The other is shaped by music, visual expression, imagination, and the feelings that words cannot always explain.

For a long time, I saw them as different parts of who I was. Today, they are the reason Malttrix exists.

01 / MY BEGINNING

Between logic and imagination.

My background gave me a strong appreciation for structure, financial discipline, and long-term thinking.

I learned to ask practical questions. How does something work? What makes it sustainable? What happens when the first excitement fades?

But I’ve never been interested in logic alone. I have always been curious about the creative side of life.

Why can a simple melody bring back a memory? Why does a certain image make us stop and feel something? How can an idea that begins in one person’s mind become meaningful to someone else?

Over time, I became more interested in the space where these two ways of thinking meet.

I didn’t want to choose between being analytical and being creative.

I wanted to understand how one could strengthen the other.

02 / WHY I STARTED MALTTRIX

I wanted to build something that could grow beyond me.

SOUNDVISIONCOGNITIONMALTTRIX
梦萃ONE SHARED FOUNDATION

An idea can begin in a moment. A piece of music. A visual concept. A question worth exploring.

But turning that idea into something lasting takes more than inspiration. It takes time, patience, care, and a way to keep moving forward. This became one of the beliefs behind Malttrix.

I wanted to create a home for original ideas. A place where different creative projects could have their own identities while benefiting from a shared foundation.

Not everything needed to become the same kind of product. Music could remain music. Visual art could speak through images. Knowledge could help people see the world differently.

But behind them, there could be a common purpose: to create something meaningful, give it room to develop, and build the systems needed to support it.

That’s why I founded Malttrix｜梦萃. Not as a finished vision, but as a place where that vision could continue to grow.

03 / WHAT I DO

I connect ideas with the systems that bring them to life.

My title is Creative Systems Architect. It may sound technical, but the idea behind it is simple.

I look at how creative ideas, brands, technology, and business processes can work together. A strong creative idea needs its own character. But it also needs the right tools, people, and structure to become something real. My work is to connect those pieces.

01

Shape an identity.

Sometimes that means shaping a brand’s identity—finding its character, direction, and own way of speaking.

02

Make room for the work.

Sometimes it means designing a process that helps creative work happen more consistently.

03

Put technology in service.

Other times, it means exploring how technology and AI can support research, production, and the everyday work behind a creative business.

I don’t believe technology should replace the human reason for creating something. I believe it should give people more room to create.

Creativity gives an idea meaning. Structure gives it the chance to last.

05 / WHAT I’M STILL LEARNING

A clear vision doesn’t mean having every answer.

I enjoy planning ahead. I like looking at the bigger picture, understanding complex problems, and finding a better way to make things work. Those habits have helped shape Malttrix.

But building something of your own has also taught me an important lesson. A good idea is only the beginning. It still needs to be tested. It needs to meet the real world. Sometimes it needs to change.

I’ve learned that it’s possible to spend too much time refining an idea before giving it the chance to grow.

I’ve also learned that independent thinking becomes stronger when you’re willing to listen, question your assumptions, and see things through someone else’s eyes.

I’m still learning how to balance careful thinking with timely action. How to protect a long-term vision while remaining open to change. And how to build creative work that means something beyond my own interests.

I don’t see Malttrix as proof that I’ve figured everything out. I see it as part of that learning process.
06 / WHAT I BELIEVE

Feel deeply. Think structurally. Build independently.

Feel deeply. Think clearly. Build with care. There are three principles I try to return to.

01

Create with meaning.

Before asking how something should look or perform, ask why it matters to the person experiencing it.

02

Build with intention.

Make thoughtful decisions. Give good ideas the time, tools, and structure they need, without making things more complicated than necessary.

03

Keep learning.

Stay curious. Listen to different perspectives. Be willing to improve an idea when the evidence points to a better way.

These principles aren’t a promise of perfection. They’re a direction I want my work to follow.

07 / A PERSONAL NOTE

This is only the beginning.

If you’ve made it this far, thank you for taking the time to learn a little about the person behind Malttrix.

I’m someone who finds meaning in creating, learning, and building things that may one day become bigger than their starting point.

I don’t know exactly what every part of Malttrix will look like in the years ahead. But I know what I want to keep working toward.

A place where imagination is taken seriously. Where technology supports human creativity. Where independent ideas have the freedom to develop their own identity. And where the work we create can continue to offer something meaningful to others.

That’s the journey I’m on. And Malttrix is where I’m building it.
