01 / Brand definition01 / 品牌定义

A world built through sound.

一个以声音构筑的情绪世界。

VANMOIRA is an emotion-led cinematic music brand and the auditory-art world within the Malttrix creative ecosystem.

It is not simply a music channel or a generic healing-music project. It has a complete sonic identity, visual language, narrative world, dual-IP architecture and Mood Capsule system.

VANMOIRA｜梵·暮汐，是一个情绪驱动的电影感音乐品牌，也是 Malttrix｜梦萃创意生态中专注于听觉艺术的世界。

它不是单纯的音乐账号，也不是泛化的疗愈音乐频道，而是拥有完整声音身份、视觉语言、叙事世界观、双 IP 架构与 Mood Capsules 情绪胶囊体系。

The sound is quiet without feeling empty. Emotional without excess. Refined without ornament. Modern without becoming cold.

这里的声音安静，但不空；深情，但不滥；精致，但不浮；现代，却不冷。

Not simply music to play.
A world to return to.这里不只是一首歌。
而是一处可以回来停留的声音空间。
01Resonance over noise.余韵而非热闹。

03Emotional world over trend.情绪场域而非流行标签。
02 / Where VANMOIRA lives02 / VANMOIRA 属于什么时刻

Between dusk and morning.

VANMOIRA belongs to the emotions that become clearer when the world becomes quieter. It does not compete for attention; it creates room for attention to soften.

It does not ask the listener to feel more intensely. It gives emotion somewhere to stay.

从暮色到晨光。

VANMOIRA 属于那些在世界安静之后，才逐渐清晰起来的情绪。它不试图争夺注意力，而是希望让注意力慢下来。

它不要求听者感受得更强烈，只是给情绪一个可以停留的地方。

05 / Four core beliefs05 / 四大品牌理念

What VANMOIRA believes.

VANMOIRA 相信什么。

01 / FEEL

Emotional honesty情绪真实

Music should carry warmth, not merely technical completion. It reflects emotions people cannot always put into words.作品必须有体温，而不只是技术完成度。音乐是情绪的镜子，而不是展示技术的舞台。

02 / SPACE

Aesthetic restraint美学克制

Elegance comes from spacing, rhythm, order and measure—not excess. Restraint is itself a form of depth.高级感来自留白、节奏、秩序与分寸，而不是堆砌。克制本身是一种深度。

03 / STAY

Healing presence疗愈陪伴

Music can accompany loneliness, exhaustion, insomnia, longing and recovery—not by demanding attention, but by staying.音乐应能陪伴孤独、疲惫、失眠、思念与恢复。它不要求听者注意它，只需要在那里。

04 / FRAME

Cinematic narrative电影叙事感

Every sound should carry image, movement, space and time—like a long film shot that lets emotion unfold.每一段声音都应带有画面、运动、空间与时间感，像电影里的长镜头，让情绪不被打断地发生。

Mission使命

Sound as a place for emotion to stay.让声音成为情绪可以停留的地方。

To build worlds of sound where emotions can be gently held, allowing listeners to come closer to themselves through listening.以声音构筑能够安放情绪的世界，让听众在聆听中更靠近自己。

Vision愿景

A wider multilingual sonic universe.建立更宽广的听觉叙事世界。

To become one of the most aesthetically distinctive emotion-led music brands in the Chinese-language cultural space, expanding through multilingual creation.成为华语语境中最具审美辨识度之一的情绪音乐品牌，并以多语言创作建立更宽广的听觉叙事世界。
